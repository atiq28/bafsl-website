Five teams, five tournament days, and one champion. Follow the full schedule, roster, ranking rules, and matchday guide for the 8th Amateur Soccer Tournament 2026.

Match schedule

Group Stage, Semi-finals & Final

Group Stage Games

Day 1 - Aug 8 Game 1: Nandonik (A) vs Ekattor FC (B) 6:30pm - 8:10pm at FO
Day 1 - Aug 8 Game 2: Joddha (C) vs Sonar Bangla (D) 8:15pm - 10:00pm at FO
Day 2 - Aug 9 Game 3: Ekattor FC (B) vs Joddha (C) 6:30pm - 8:10pm at Shoreline
Day 2 - Aug 9 Game 4: Sonar Bangla (D) vs Dhumketu (E) 8:15pm - 10:00pm at Shoreline
Day 3 - Aug 15 Game 5: Dhumketu (E) vs Nandonik (A) 7:00am - 9:00am at FO / Shoreline / Crittenden

Only one team is eliminated after the group stage.

Semi-finals

Day 4 - Aug 16 Game 6: Rank 1 vs Rank 4 6:10pm - 7:50pm at FO
Day 4 - Aug 16 Game 7: Rank 2 vs Rank 3 8:00pm - 10:00pm at FO

Final

Day 5 - Aug 22 Game 8: Winner of Game 6 vs Winner of Game 7 6:00pm - 10:00pm at FO

Team Ranking

  • Win: 3 points
  • Draw: 1 point
  • Top four teams qualify for the semi-finals.

Tie-breaker Order

  1. Goal difference
  2. Total goals scored
  3. Head-to-head record
  4. Total goals conceded
  5. Fair play points
  6. Penalty shootout before the next scheduled game day

Matchday Guide

  • Playing teams are responsible for field setup and cleanup.
  • The alphabetically first team sets up; the opponent returns posts to storage.
  • Teams should bring their own water, Gatorade, and banana.
  • Only submitted roster players may play. Failure may result in a -1 point penalty.
